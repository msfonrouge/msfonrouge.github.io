Campaign reports without copying numbers

A marketing team sent many email campaigns: some to customers, some to employees. Every report meant opening each campaign and copying the same numbers into a spreadsheet by hand.

The numbers were always the same ones: how many people got the email, how many deliveries worked, how many opened it, how many clicked, and how many unsubscribed.

I wrote a program that opens each campaign, reads those numbers, and writes them into a spreadsheet, one row per campaign. There is one version for customer campaigns and one for internal news.

It is a small tool. But it turned an afternoon of copying into a file that is ready when you need it, and it never makes a typing mistake.
