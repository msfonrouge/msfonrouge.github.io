Selling game codes online

In 2017 I set up an online store that sold codes for video games.

I started on WordPress, with a shop plugin and an add-on that was supposed to deliver the codes. It did not work the way the store needed.

So I moved the store to Shopify. I imported the products and ran test orders to check the whole flow, from the cart to the code.

What I take from it: choose the platform that already solves the hardest part of your business, even if it means starting again.
