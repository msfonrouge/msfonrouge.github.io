Finding what to sell, and at what price

The idea was simple: buy products cheap from a supplier, and sell them where people already buy. The hard part was knowing which products were worth it.

The supplier, a computer wholesaler, had an API. I downloaded its full list, about 1,500 products, with stock and price in dollars, and converted the prices to pesos.

Then the program searched each product on Mercado Libre. For every one it found the cheapest new listing, how much that listing sold, the link, and what Mercado Libre would keep in commission and shipping.

The result was one spreadsheet that answered the real question: which products sell well, and still leave money after the supplier and the fees. Now I use it to decide what to buy.
