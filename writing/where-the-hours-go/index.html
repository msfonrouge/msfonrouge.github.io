Where the hours really go

A sales team felt overloaded, and everyone agreed on the cause: writing quotes took too long. Before building anything to speed them up, I wanted to see it.

I put together 61 working days from the records the team already left behind: messages, documents, the sales system, email and calendars. Then I made a simple page that shows, day by day, where the time went.

Quotes took about six minutes each. They were not the problem. More than half of the working day left no trace in any system at all. And 128 real client requests had waited more than a day for an answer.

That changed what to fix next. It is the part of this work I like most: measuring first, so you build the thing that matters and not the thing everyone assumed.
