How I work

  • Measure first. I look at the real records before I build anything, so we fix the problem that costs the most.
  • Small and safe. Every change gets a test run first, and a way to undo it.
  • People stay in charge. The software does the repetitive part. Anything unclear goes to a person.
  • Plain language. You hear what changed and what it means for you, not the technical details.
